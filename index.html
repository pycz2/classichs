Classic HS

Hearthstone as it was before Naxxramas: all 382 cards, nine classes, the original tutorial — and online play with a friend straight from the browser, no servers.

Play now Source code

Free. Works offline against the bot. No account.

Everything from the original

🃏

All 382 cards

Every Basic and Classic card with its original values and mechanics: Secrets, Choose One, Overload, Combo, Enrage, Deathrattle.

🤖

Training with a bot

Practise any deck against a simple bot of any class. Works without the internet.

🔗

Online without servers

WebRTC connects two browsers directly. Exchange a text code in any messenger — and play.

🎓

The original tutorial

Six missions as Jaina: Hogger, Millhouse, Lorewalker Cho, King Mukla, Hemet and Illidan.

📚

Collection and decks

All cards unlocked, unlimited decks saved in the browser, deck codes compatible with Hearthstone.

📊

Deck tracker and history

Cards left in your deck with the chance to draw each one, what the opponent played, and the full move log.

🧪

Debug table

Set up any position and test any card or combo against the bot or for both sides.

🌍

8 languages, sound, voice

English, Русский, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português, Italiano, Polski. Legendary minions say their names.

Play with a friend in three steps

  1. 1Press “Create Game” and send the invite code to a friend.
  2. 2The friend presses “Join Game” and sends back the answer code.
  3. 3Paste the answer and press “Connect”. The browsers talk directly.
