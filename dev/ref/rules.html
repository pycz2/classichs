rules.json

Everything about a match, a deck, the Arena and the ladder that is not a card. Schema: schema/rules.schema.json. Whole numbers only (it is hashed canonically, format.md §3). Values below are the Classic pack's (packs/classic/rules.json), which match what the game and the server do today. The engine knows no game of its own: everything game-specific it uses (the coin, champions and their powers, classes, rarities, starter decks, the Arena) comes from here and from cards.json; an optional field the pack leaves out takes the engine's default (the Classic value in these tables, champion_health 30, queue_steps 5000, champions_offered 3, …; no coin, no starter rarities).

start#

FieldClassicMeaning
first_player"random"Who goes first (the game seed decides).
hand[3, 4]Cards drawn by the first and the second player.
coin"GAME_005"The card the second player gets after the mulligan (null: none).
mulligantruePlayers may replace any of their opening cards once.
first_player_drawstrueThe first player draws a card at the start of their first turn (false: only from the second turn on).

These are the levers on the first player's advantage: more opening cards for the second player (hand), the coin, and the first player's first draw. Neonfall gives the second player five cards ([3, 5]): with [3, 4] the first player won 56–60 % of bot games, with [3, 5] 47–53 % (packs/neon/DESIGN.md §9).

bot#

How the engine's bot (rust/engine/src/bot.rs, greedy: it takes the action whose result it values most, until ending the turn is worth as much) plays this pack. Optional; Classic leaves it out, and its bot stays the recorded one (parity).

FieldClassicMeaning
trap0What the bot counts for each of its own traps in play (a card in hand counts 1). At 0 it never plays a trap; at 2 it plays one when nothing better is left to do (Neonfall).
depth12: before ending its turn, the bot also tries two actions in a row, so it plays a card that pays off only with the next one (the coin, then a minion; a spell to turn on a Chain). Turn ends cost about as many look-aheads as there are actions squared.
brain(always)true: the game's bot at a level (easy, medium, hard) thinks with rust/brain (Classic's bot: it guesses the hidden cards and looks ahead) instead of the engine's greedy bot. Classic gets it by its id; Naxxramas sets it.

fatigue#

{"enabled": true, "first": 1, "step": 1}: drawing from an empty deck deals first damage, then step more each time.

behaviour#

Rules Hearthstone changed over the years; a pack names the ones it plays by (all off when missing: Classic, Naxxramas and Goblins vs Gnomes keep their recorded games; a later pack keeps the earlier pack's flags unless a patch changed that behaviour).

FieldClassicMeaning
countered_no_after_spellfalsetrue: a countered spell is not cast for "after you cast a spell" hooks (no after_spell event: Wild Pyromancer, Flamewaker; Hearthstone until patch 9.2.0.21517 of 2017: Kobolds & Catacombs sets it false again). "Whenever you cast a spell" (play) still sees it.
full_hand_return_diesfalsetrue: a minion returned to a full hand (Sap, Youthful Brewmaster…) dies as any other: its Deathrattle, the graveyard, DIED_TURN (Kel'Thuzad, Feugen and Stalagg, Resurrect; patch 2.4.0.8311). false: it is set aside.
cost_in_orderfalsetrue: a card's cost changes apply in the order they came — its cost enchantments when given, the cost auras when their source came into play, its player's cost mods when made — after its own cost_mod, the cost_first auras before all (by rank: Summoning Portal 1); 0 at least at the end (the 2016 rulebook, rule M5: Emperor Thaurissan after Naga Sea Witch makes a card cost 4; from patch 9.0.0.20457 of 2017 Naga Sea Witch, Corpse Widow and Emerald Hive Queen come right after Summoning Portal: their scripts' cost_first = 2 in Knights of the Frozen Throne). false: enchantments, then auras, then cost mods.
after_spell_from_beforefalsetrue: a spell's after_spell event (its since field: the play order it was played at) is heard only by what was in play before the spell was played: a minion it summoned does not react to it (patch 9.2.0.21517 of 2017: "Wild Pyromancer no longer triggers from Mindgames"; Kobolds & Catacombs). false: by all in play when it resolved.
lifesteal_one_healfalsetrue: the Lifesteal damage of one hook (a spell, a Battlecry, a Deathrattle…) heals its champion once, when the hook ends, for all it dealt, one heal per source (patch 10.0.0.22611 of 2017: "multi-target effects now apply healing as one heal"; Kobolds & Catacombs). false: each hit heals at once (Knights of the Frozen Throne).
replaced_weapon_diesfalsetrue: a weapon replaced by a new one is destroyed as any other: its Deathrattle and a death event (Death's Bite, Grave Shambler; hearthstone.wiki.gg, Weapon). false: it goes to the graveyard without them.

player_hooks#

The name of a hook set of the pack's scripts ("player:og") whose on hooks see every event once for each player, me being that player's champion (a new champion takes over): the counts of a game no card in play keeps ("spells you've cast this game", "Totems you've summoned this game"; g.add_count, g.count), and what happens "when the game starts" (an on.game_start hook: after the mulligan, before the coin; api.md, events). Optional; none in Classic.

every_race#

A race (of races) that is every race: a card of it counts as a Beast, a Murloc, a Pirate… for every script (g.race and g.info(card).race give a value == to every race name, not to ""; a cost mod of a race applies to it). Optional; The Witchwood names ALL (Nightmare Amalgam: "This is an Elemental, Mech, Demon, Murloc, Dragon, Beast, Pirate and Totem").

classes, races, rarities, no_rarity_tier, sets#

See format.md §5. Classic: nine playable classes (DRUID … WARRIOR, champions HERO_01 … HERO_09), NEUTRAL (neutral), DREAM and DEMONHUNTER (special cards only); races BEAST DEMON DRAGON MECHANICAL MURLOC PIRATE TOTEM UNDEAD; rarities FREE and COMMON (tier common), RARE, EPIC, LEGENDARY; sets VANILLA (the collection), MISSIONS, EXPERT1, LEGACY, HERO_SKINS, CORE. Naxxramas (packs/naxx): the same and NAXX, both collections (VANILLA, NAXX) in its constructed and Arena modes.

mechanics#

Pack name → {"engine": <engine id>} (format.md §4). Classic maps every Hearthstone mechanic and every state tag to the engine vocabulary while keeping the Hearthstone names (D7).

arena#

The draft as rust/engine/src/arena_draft.rs does it:

FieldClassicMeaning
sets["VANILLA"]Card pool (collectible, no champions).
champions_offered3Champions of different classes to choose from.
classesthe nineClasses that can be offered.
picks30Picks (the deck size).
offer3Different cards per pick, all of one tier.
tier_weightscommon 765, rare 140, epic 75, legendary 20Chance of a pick's tier, per mille (engine: weight / 1000.0).
special_picks[1, 10, 20, 30]Pick numbers (1-based) that use special_tier_weights.
special_tier_weightsrare 650, epic 270, legendary 80
class_weight, neutral_weight3, 2Weight of a class card vs a neutral card within a tier (1.5 : 1).
copies0Copies limit of an Arena deck (0: none).
wins, losses12, 3A run ends at this many wins or losses.

Draft algorithm (for the engine to keep): offer champions_offered champions, each drawn uniformly (randi_range) from the classes left, in the listed order; for each pick, draw a tier by the weights (x = randf(), subtract each tier's weight/1000 in the listed order, the first that drops below 0; the last on rounding), then draw offer different cards of that tier, each by weight among the remaining (x = randf() * total, walk the list). The tier of a card is its rarity's tier; no rarity → no_rarity_tier. All from the draft seed (rng.rs).

ranks[].streak_bonustrue at 25–6 (default false)The streak bonus applies at this rank.
ranks[].emblem{card: id} / {image: "ui/…"} / nullThe emblem: a card's art in a ring (Classic: the rank's namesake card, as ui/server_view.gd RANK_CARDS), a picture, or the client's crest.
