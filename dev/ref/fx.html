Visual effects (fx)

The client has a fixed catalog of named effects (D16). A pack never draws anything itself: a card names effects in its fx field and the client plays them. An unknown name plays the slot's default. Machine-readable list: schema/fx.json. The code: ui/table_fx.gd (effects on the table), ui/match_view.gd (bolts, missiles, shake, shatter, burn, fatigue, reveal, banner, lunge, pulse), ui/art.gd (ELEMENT_COLORS).

json"fx": {"element": "fire", "cast": "bolt", "hit": "hurt"}        // Fireball
"fx": {"element": "arcane", "cast": "missiles", "hit": "hurt"}  // Arcane Missiles
"fx": {"play": "battlecry", "death": "skull"}                  // a minion

States are drawn from engine keywords, not from fx: GUARD (a shield frame), BARRIER (a golden bubble), STEALTH, FROZEN (ice), DOUBLE_ATTACK, HASTE, IMMUNE, SPELL_POWER, the sleeping mark, the trigger and death marks.

3. Classic: how its cards got their fx#

The Classic client picks a spell's colour from its picture plan (Art.design), and plays hits by event. packs/classic/cards.patch.json writes that down per card as a starting point (dumped from Art._subject with headless Godot):

  • element (spells and powers): the element of the name's motif (fire, frost, arcane, …), else the class's: Mage arcane, Priest and Paladin holy, Warlock shadow, Druid and Hunter nature, Shaman lightning, Rogue poison, Warrior steel, Dream moon.
  • hit, from the English text: damage → hurt; restore/heal → heal; +Attack /+Health → buff; Armor → armor; Divine Shield, Taunt, Stealth, Immune → gain; destroy → destroy; transform → smoke; return → whoosh; silence → silence; freeze without damage → freeze; summon → portal; mana → gain.
  • cast: missiles when the text deals damage at random; bolt for the rest except draw, summon, mana, secrets and plain emblems (nothing flies).
  • Minions: play: battlecry with BATTLECRY, death: skull with DEATHRATTLE.

The client reads fx from the pack (TableFx.named, ui/table_fx.gd): a name that is not in the catalog counts as none given, and the slot's default plays. cast: none sends no bolt, hit adds its effect to what the change shows (none: nothing), play: portal always shows, battlecry and skull keep their conditions (the play hook acted; an unsilenced death hook), death: destroy always shows.
